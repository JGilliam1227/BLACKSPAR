Issue 001 The AI Discipline Gap

The Signal

AI isn't exposing a technology gap. It's exposing a business discipline gap.

The companies struggling with AI adoption aren't behind because they lack tools. They're behind because their processes, data, ownership, and decision rules were already unclear. AI didn't create the constraint. It revealed it.

Two companies buy the identical software and get opposite results. One had clear ownership and clean inputs. The other had an expensive rumor of a process. The tool didn't decide the outcome — the discipline did.

The Line

Above the line: the debate over which model to buy. Below the line, where it actually matters: whether your business is disciplined enough for any of them to help. The market is loud about the first question and silent about the second.

The Executive Move

Before approving any new AI tool this quarter, pick the process you'd point it at and ask one question: could a new hire run it from documentation alone? If not, the constraint is discipline, not tooling — fix that first.
